MADE FOR YOUR REMOTE

One press. A better recap.

Watching: Down focuses the bottom controls: Previous, Play/Pause, Previously, and Next. Select the Previously logo to recap scenes before the playback position.

Recap feed: Down advances and marks Remember after two seconds, before seven seconds of playback; an earlier Down just skips. Staying for seven seconds automatically marks Not remember and adds context while the current clip stays visible. Pauses, buffering and popups do not count. Up revisits the previous clip and requests more context. Left opens scene notes; Right opens the map at the clip source timestamp; Menu (M on a laptop) opens AI details. Enter/Space pauses. Back resumes the original movie.

The story map reads characters and relationships directly from your SQLite database, with no AI calls. Your playback position unlocks earlier appearances and connections, including skipped scenes. AI reads timed transcripts and source frames to classify scenes and edit recap clips on demand. Remember advances the existing queue without an AI call. Character and connection selections add clips; Not remember asks for different context.

Recaps cover every scene before your playback position, including skipped scenes and unfinished titles. Future scenes are excluded. Future map facts and untimed biographies stay hidden. Successful AI results and clips are cached. Without AI there is no invented recap or substitute recommendation. Every provider response is saved on your laptop in runtime/logs/ai-responses.jsonl.
